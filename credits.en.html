Credits and licences

Last revised: 2 November 2026

Braise stands on other people’s work. This screen names them, because a missing credit is noticed and an uncredited photographer asked for nothing.

Photographs

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons, under Creative Commons licences or in the public domain, and from Pexels, under the Pexels licence. Those credited “Braise” are the publisher’s own and ship with the app.

Every photo shown carries its author’s name and its licence; for image-bank photos, the credit links in one tap to the source page, where the full terms appear. No image enters the app without those mentions — an automated test checks it on every build.

Software

The app is written with Flutter and Dart, published by Google under the three-clause BSD licence.

  • flutter_riverpod — state management, MIT licence.
  • go_router — navigation, BSD-3-Clause licence.
  • shared_preferences — local storage, BSD-3-Clause licence.
  • cached_network_image — photo caching, MIT licence.
  • url_launcher — opening credit pages and your email app, BSD-3-Clause licence.
  • share_plus — share sheet, BSD-3-Clause licence.
  • file_selector — choosing the file to import, BSD-3-Clause licence.
  • path_provider — where the journal is kept, BSD-3-Clause licence.
  • wakelock_plus — screen awake in cook mode, BSD-3-Clause licence.
  • flutter_svg — illustrations, MIT licence.
  • google_fonts — typefaces, Apache 2.0 licence.
