Nutrition figures are estimates

Calories and macronutrients are computed by applying a reference food table to the recipe quantities. They are orders of magnitude, useful for comparing dishes or adjusting amounts. They are not measurements, and they are in no sense medical data.

The app deliberately shows a margin of error and names the ingredients it cannot evaluate, precisely because a bare number would suggest a precision it does not have.

Nutrition tracking, which is optional, adds up these same estimates: a day’s totals, and what is left before a target, are orders of magnitude, all the rougher when amounts are noted by eye. It is neither a medical device nor medical advice.

If you follow a prescribed diet, or are pregnant, diabetic or under treatment, ask a health professional rather than a cooking app.

Allergens are inferred, not guaranteed

Allergen labels are derived automatically from the ingredient catalogue. They may miss an allergen present in a processed product you buy, in an industrial preparation, or introduced by a substitution you make yourself.

The allergens of ingredients you create yourself are the ones you enter. Until you say anything about them, they remain “not specified” — never “none”.

The allergies you tick in At home set recipes aside based on their listed ingredients: any recipe with an ingredient that contains the allergen, or may contain it depending on the brand, drops out of what Braise suggests. This filter only sees what is listed; it never replaces reading the labels.

If you have a severe allergy, always check the full ingredient list and the labels of the products you use. The “exclude allergens” filter is a search aid, not a safety guarantee.

Kitchen safety

Times, temperatures and sensory cues given in the recipes are indicative. They depend on your equipment, the real size of your ingredients and your oven. Cooking meat, poultry, eggs and fish through remains your judgement.

Cook mode keeps the screen awake while it is open. It watches nothing on your behalf: a timer going off is no substitute for someone in the kitchen.
