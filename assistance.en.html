Support

Last updated: 12 November 2026

A question, a problem, an idea for Braise Cuisine? Write to the publisher, Emile KOFFI: koffi972@gmail.com. Every message is read by the person who makes the app.

Writing to us

“Your feedback”, in the Me tab, opens your email app with our address and a subject already filled in. You can also write directly to koffi972@gmail.com.

For a problem, tell us what you were doing, what happened, and your phone model with its system version. A screenshot often helps.

Photos don’t show

Recipe photos are downloaded the first time they appear, then kept on the device. With no network, a photo you have never seen gives way to a coloured gradient; it will appear next time you are online. Everything else — recipes, quantities, calculations — works offline.

Moving to a new phone

Braise has no account: your data stays on the device. To take it with you, “Export a backup”, in the Me tab, creates a file you can send wherever you like; on the new phone, “Import a backup” reads it back. Your device backup, if turned on, carries it over too.

Deleting my data

Uninstalling Braise erases everything it keeps on the device. Device backups follow your own settings. The publisher holds no data about you: there is nothing to delete on our side, except emails you have written to us — just ask.

Allergens and nutrition

Braise flags the allergens of the listed ingredients and estimates nutrition values from reference tables. These are guides: they never replace reading the labels (see the terms of use).
